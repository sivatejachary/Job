About the role

As an AI/ML Engineer at Northstar AI, you'll collaborate with product and engineering teams to turn data and research into dependable machine-learning features. You'll work across experimentation, model evaluation, API integration, and production monitoring.

What you'll do

  • Prepare datasets, build data pipelines, and perform exploratory data analysis.
  • Train, evaluate, and improve machine-learning and deep-learning models.
  • Develop reproducible experiments and measure model quality using suitable metrics.
  • Build inference APIs and integrate models into application workflows.
  • Collaborate with software engineers, product managers, and domain experts.
  • Document experiments, monitor model performance, and help address data or model drift.

Required qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, AI/ML, Data Science, Statistics, or a related field; equivalent project experience is welcome.
  • Working knowledge of Python, SQL, data structures, and software development fundamentals.
  • Experience with NumPy, Pandas, scikit-learn, and at least one of PyTorch or TensorFlow.
  • Understanding of supervised learning, model validation, overfitting, and evaluation metrics.
  • Ability to explain technical decisions and collaborate effectively.

  • Collaborative engineering environment and flexible hybrid work.
  • Learning budget and time for technical development.
